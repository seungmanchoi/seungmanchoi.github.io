3-Min Brain Games

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

3-Min Brain Games has no sign-up or login and no server of our own. Your game records, streaks, badges, and settings are stored only on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use an advertising identifier as described below.

1. Overview

3-Min Brain Games is a daily brain-game app developed by Seungman Choi (bundle ID: com.seungmanchoi.brain3min). The app has no accounts and no server that stores your data.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

  • Game records: scores and play history.
  • Streaks and badges: daily attendance streaks and earned badges.
  • Settings: your app preferences.

This data is stored only on your device (AsyncStorage) and is never sent to us. It is erased when you delete the app.

3. Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and rewarded formats). Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android), approximate location (based on your IP address), and device information to deliver and measure ads.

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): the IDFA is used only if you allow tracking.
  • EEA and UK (GDPR): we show a consent screen (Google UMP) before ads load and respect your choice.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices anytime in the app settings or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. Analytics And Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage events (such as a game being started or completed) and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. This data contains no personally identifiable information.

5. Notifications And Sharing

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled only on your device; there is no push server. Your results are shared only when you choose to send them yourself through the OS share sheet.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: advertising. See Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: anonymous usage analytics and crash reports.

7. Not Medical Advice

Game scores are for entertainment only. They are not intended for medical use or diagnosis.

8. Children

3-Min Brain Games is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact blueng.choi@gmail.com.

개인정보처리방침

하루 3분 두뇌게임은 회원가입·로그인이 없고 자체 서버도 없습니다. 게임 기록, 연속 출석, 배지, 설정은 기기에만 저장됩니다. 앱은 Google AdMob을 통해 광고를 표시하며, 아래 설명과 같이 광고 식별자를 사용할 수 있습니다.

하루 3분 두뇌게임은 Seungman Choi가 개발한 매일 두뇌게임 앱입니다(번들 ID: com.seungmanchoi.brain3min). 사용자 계정이나 데이터를 저장하는 서버는 없습니다.

  • 연속 출석과 배지: 매일 출석 기록과 획득한 배지.
  • 설정: 앱 설정.

이 데이터는 기기(AsyncStorage)에만 저장되며 우리에게 전송되지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 모두 지워집니다.

  • EEA 및 영국(GDPR): 광고를 불러오기 전에 동의 화면(Google UMP)을 표시하고 선택을 따릅니다.

4. 분석 및 진단

앱은 Firebase Analytics로 게임 시작·완료 같은 익명 사용 이벤트를 파악하고, Firebase Crashlytics로 충돌 보고서를 받습니다. 이 데이터에는 개인 식별 정보가 없습니다.

5. 알림 및 공유

매일 리마인더는 기기에서만 예약되는 로컬 알림이며 푸시 서버는 없습니다. 결과 공유는 사용자가 OS 공유 시트로 직접 보낼 때만 이루어집니다.

  • Firebase Analytics 및 Crashlytics: 익명 사용 분석 및 충돌 보고.

게임 점수는 오락용일 뿐이며 의료 목적이나 진단용이 아닙니다.

1日3分 脳トレゲームは Seungman Choi が開発した毎日の脳トレゲームアプリです(バンドル ID: com.seungmanchoi.brain3min)。ユーザーアカウントやデータを保存するサーバーはありません。

毎日のリマインダーは端末内でのみ予約されるローカル通知で、プッシュサーバーはありません。結果の共有は、ユーザーが OS の共有シートから自分で送信した場合にのみ行われます。
